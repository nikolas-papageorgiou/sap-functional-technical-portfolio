# Glossary

English SAP term, the Greek term shown on screen, and the meaning in plain language.
A Greek screen term is listed only after it has been confirmed; otherwise the cell says "to be confirmed".

| English term | Greek screen term | Meaning | Lesson |
|--------------|-------------------|---------|--------|
| Notification | Γνωστοποίηση | A record that something needs attention: what was observed or requested, where, by whom and when. | Kickoff |
