# Company profile: Meltemi Grid S.A.

> Meltemi Grid S.A. is a fictional company. Every figure, name and place in this document is invented.

## What the company does

Meltemi Grid S.A. is the electricity distribution system operator of a fictional country. It owns and operates the medium- and low-voltage network, connects new customers to the grid, repairs faults and reads meters. It does not sell electricity: suppliers do, and they use Meltemi Grid's network to reach their customers.

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| Supply points served | about 1.2 million |
| Regions | North, South, Islands |
| Head office | in the South region |

## Regions

| Region | Character | Typical challenge |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| North | Mountainous, long overhead lines | Weather-related faults in winter |
| South | Dense urban network, mostly underground | High volume of new connection requests |
| Islands | Small isolated networks | Long travel times for field crews |

## Business roles

These roles appear in the solutions of this repository.

| Role | What the person does |
|------|----------------------|
| Call-centre agent | Takes fault reports and requests from customers |
| Dispatcher | Assesses incoming reports and assigns them to field crews |
| Field crew foreman | Leads the crew on site and reports what was found and done |
| Connections clerk | Handles new connection requests from application to energisation |
| Network planner | Designs network extensions and decides on technical feasibility |
| Maintenance planner | Plans and schedules maintenance work |
